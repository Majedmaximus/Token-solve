◈ TRACE / Signed Request Verifier

Check the request.
Not just the message.

Free browser-only pilot: sign payment instructions and detect changed details. No account, wallet connection, blockchain payment, token purchase or verification fee.

Signing and checking are free. The requested amount is a payment instruction, not a TRACE fee. No transfer is executed. TRON/TRC20, Solana/SPL and EVM instructions are supported; Bitcoin, XRP, bank and card payments are not supported.

A valid signature proves control of a key, not a person's identity or honesty. Obtain the expected key fingerprint through an independently trusted channel. Never trust a fingerprint just because it arrives with the request.

This tool does not check balances, execute transfers, confirm token authenticity or all token decimals, detect stolen signing keys, check registry status or prevent reuse on its own. This public pilot has no cancellation registry, shared request status or one-time acknowledgement service. Do not use it as proof that a request is unspent or uncancelled. No guarantee of payment safety.
