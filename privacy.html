Privacy & pilot limitations.

This notice describes the current browser-only pilot. It is not a certification of legal compliance or an independent security audit.

What stays in your browser

Signing keys are temporary, non-exportable Web Crypto keys held in the page's memory. Refreshing or closing the tab loses them. The application does not send your private key, signed instructions or verification inputs to an application backend.

Contacts you explicitly save contain labels and public-key fingerprints in this origin's localStorage. They persist across reloads until removed. They are not encrypted or tamper-proof. Use Remove selected contact to delete a saved contact; clearing this site's browser data removes all local contacts. Exported contact files can expose your labels and relationships: review before sharing.

Hosting and network requests

The pilot has no application analytics, account signup, payment processing or application cookies. It downloads its own static files. The hosting provider receives normal web requests and may keep access logs, including IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement for the provider's practices.

What verification means

A signature proves control of a key and integrity of signed fields. It does not certify a person's identity, honesty, available funds, token legitimacy or payment safety. Obtain the expected fingerprint independently. This public pilot does not consult a cancellation registry, execute payments or prevent reuse across services.

Project contact

The site is published from the project repository. Do not post private keys, identity documents or confidential requests in public issues. A dedicated confidential reporting channel and operating-entity details have not yet been established.

No token sale, public token contract address, investment return or exchange approval is offered by this pilot.
